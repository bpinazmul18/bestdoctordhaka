# BestDoctorDhaka Product Requirements

## Product Overview
BestDoctorDhaka.com is a Bangladesh-focused healthcare directory, initially focused on Dhaka.

The product is directory-first, not telemedicine-first. It helps users discover doctors, hospitals, diagnostic centers, specialties, locations, and useful healthcare information.

## Vision
Build a trusted healthcare discovery platform for Bangladesh.

Core value loop:
Google Search → BestDoctorDhaka → provider discovery → profile → Call / WhatsApp / Contact → provider value → featured listing / subscription.

## Primary Users
### Patients / Visitors
- Find doctors and specialists
- Find hospitals and diagnostic centers
- Browse by specialty and location
- Search and filter providers
- View useful provider information
- Contact providers
- Read useful healthcare information

### Doctors

- Featured visibility

### Hospitals and Diagnostic Centers
Eventually:

- Leads
- Premium visibility

### Administrators

- Moderate reviews

## Out of Scope for Foundation
Do not implement during Phase 0:
- Business features
- Reviews
- BMDC verification workflows
- Payments
- Subscriptions
- Appointments
- Patient/provider dashboards
- Advanced analytics
- Mobile app
- Microservices
- Kubernetes
- OpenSearch / Elasticsearch

## Trust and Data Accuracy
Never invent:
- Doctor credentials
- BMDC registration numbers
- Verification status
- Hospital affiliations
- Diagnostic services
- Reviews
- Patient experiences
- Medical claims

Unknown information must remain unknown, pending, or unverified as appropriate.

BestDoctorDhaka must never be represented as BMDC or as a government verification authority.

## SEO
SEO is a core acquisition channel.

Important public pages:
- Doctor profiles
- Hospital profiles
- Diagnostic profiles
- Specialty pages
- Location pages
- Health articles

Avoid thin pages, duplicate pages, keyword stuffing, fabricated healthcare content, and low-value programmatic SEO.

## Monetization and Trust
Paid placement must never be represented as medical superiority or official endorsement.

## Product Priorities
1. Accuracy
2. Trust
3. User usefulness
4. Search discoverability
5. Performance
6. Maintainability
7. Monetization
8. Scale

Major product decisions require Product Owner approval.
